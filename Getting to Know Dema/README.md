# 🙈 TL;DR (<1min read)

* **What is Dema?** A decentralized e-commerce marketplace governed by its user that saves shoppers and sellers while acquiring users by giving away free ownership equity.
* **What's the Problem?** Marketplaces impose 20%+ in selling & ad fees starving causing more sellers to fail as they're deprived of capital via attention bidding wars. With fewer sellers in control of more SKUs, even as greater numbers enter, these additional costs raise shopper prices. All of this is caused by users' inability to govern marketplaces.
* **Vetted with Users/Pent up demand:** Live prototype demonstrations with >100 sellers & >200 shoppers received high desire positive feedback.
* **Shop Anywhere, Day one Discounts Shopping Experience:** Shoppers loved that they can browse from any site & get at least a 5% discount, free ownership & other benefits.
* **No Effort, No Storefront, 2x profits Seller Experience:** Sellers integrate their existing stores with Dema in minutes. With $0 forever marketing & no selling fees, sellers told us Dema they more than double profits. Loved that they receive free ownership.
* **Target Audience:** Dema's target audience includes crypto enthusiasts & cost-conscious shoppers.
* **Why Free Ownership:** Dema gives away equity to users, reducing acquisition costs and aligning incentives with shoppers and sellers.
