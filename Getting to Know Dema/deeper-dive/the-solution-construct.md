# 💡 The Solution Construct

## A marketplace owned and governed by its users

Dema's solution to the current state of marketplaces was borne from four elements:

1. Create **Day ONE incentives & benefits** for sellers and shoppers
2. Create a UX so **shoppers and seller have zero to minimal habit changes**.
3. **Use free ownership & referrals to reduce shopper & seller acquisition costs**&#x20;
4. Deliver progressive governance

While it's obvious why we need to deliver the above; let's explain the "CAC" one a little more.

## **Why Reducing CAC &&#x20;**<mark style="color:blue;">**FREE**</mark>**&#x20;ownership go hand in hand?**

**It's all about incentive alignment!** Raising capital to spend on massive marketing budgets & acquisition costs is one of the primary problems startups face. Dema's ownership model is designed so sellers & shoppers will want to promote Dema.&#x20;

Almost all ownership sellers & shoppers receive comes from the [**"sell-2-earn"** & **"shop-2-earn"**](#user-content-fn-1)[^1] model. And only when you shop on Dema, are you able to govern Dema. This provides other utility & benefits which includes sellers & shoppers getting substantial financial returns if they're able to sell their earned Dema ownership.

**So what else did we need to do? And why?**

1. **Making it easy for Shoppers and Sellers to use Dema right away**: Our UX mantra is "don't change seller & shopper behavior". This not only reduces the cost of acquisition (CAC) (Dema would have to spend gobs of money trying to redirect traffic away from other marketplaces) but we'd be fighting a nearly impossible uphill battle for  attention; one of the very things we espouse against! It also simply does not work against behemoths with established deep pockets. We'd be at the mercy of advertising algorithms: A no-go! So we designed a seamless UX for both sellers and shoppers.
2. **Only users can govern**: Ensuring no non-user (e.g.: operator or investors) can raise fees & change policies limits how Dema can be used as a tool for profit accumulation. Even sellers & shoppers ownership (making them co-opted investors) is distinct from their ability to govern. Using Dema determines how you can govern it. Ownership in its delivered value does not confer that privilege.
3. **Sellers & shoppers need an incentive to use Dema on Day ONE**: On day one, we reduce selling fees and pass some of the fees we collected to the shopper. So on day one, even without sellers reducing their prices, shoppers get a discount on any item priced the same at a marketplace. **Over time, we'll reduce the shopper incentive down to zero** and in turn collect lower selling fees always preserving the no-profit mantra.
