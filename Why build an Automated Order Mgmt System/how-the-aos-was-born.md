# ⚙️ How the AOS was born

Dema developed the AOS (automated order mgmt system) to relieve the business partners from the manual process of fulfilling shopper requests. The AOS facilitates every single request the shopper may trigger: From purchasing items, to canceling an ordered item's purchase, to returns, tracking an order, etc…. This essential component facilitates the near-instant experience for shoppers while eliminating most of the hurdles the strategic business partners might have had to support Dema's goals.

### 🔭 Expansion plans

Even as Dema brings on sellers to operate as a full-fledged two-sided marketplace, the "surrogate sellers" and the AOS will bridge the gap when Shoppers are looking for items for which a seller has not signed up with Dema yet. While initially, the AOS will apply to Amazon, it will expand to cover other marketplaces in multiple geographies.&#x20;
