# 🩳 Recap Summary

* Dema's marketplace economics will drastically reduce costs for sellers while addressing the "[cold-start](why-this-user-experience.md#the-cold-start-problem)" problem.
* Dema uses transparent on-chain contracts to maintain purchase fund escrow to fulfill its role as an "[arbiter of trust](./#the-two-roles-of-a-marketplace)" until the shoppers receive their items and the purchase order lifecycle is completed.
* Strategic ("surrogate seller") business partners use their contributed liquidity to fulfill the orders on the supply side to drop-ship the items the shoppers purchased from the very same sellers the shoppers were told they would get it from.
* This "one-sided" marketplace operation will continue even as the direct Amazon sellers join Dema to achieve full-fledged two-sided marketplace operation. This also avoids the need to wait for a large starting base of sellers.
* The Automated Ordering System (AOS) scripts caters to the strategic business partners need to automate order management functions: From adding, validating & editing an address to order placement, auditing an order, locating & retrieving order details, cancellations, returns and tracking requests.
* Dema shoppers do not have to deal with the "[high switching cost](why-this-user-experience.md#the-switching-pain-problem)" problem. At first, Dema provides a familiar browsing experience at Amazon and soon after launch, Dema will expand to include the same experience at additional marketplaces.
