# 🤳 How do Dema's shoppers get their stuff?

To solve the cold-start problem and ensure there's a nearly unlimited supply of SKUs, Dema developed a strategic set of business partners who source products from the same Amazon sellers that the Shoppers purchase through the Dema app.

### Sequence of Events

When a Dema shopper places an order, it is routed to these businesses who in turn acquire the items from the sellers on Amazon & ship them directly to the shopper. This strategy,  known as a one-sided marketplace is made possible by the strategic business partners. They provide liquidity to purchase the items shoppers want from Amazon.&#x20;

It also ensure Dema does NOT have to wait until a massive number of direct sellers are recruited to turn on the two-sided marketplace. Dema can start operating as a single-sided marketplace with the help of these strategic partners while enabling shoppers to have unlimited inventory to choose from.
