# 🎆 How shopping at Dema works?

A Dema Shopper’s browsing experience differs from traditional marketplaces. Sellers have no stores to sell from at Dema. Instead, shoppers use Dema apps to browse items on other marketplaces like Amazon, or others.&#x20;

As a result, sellers do NOT have to setup stores on Dema and shoppers can browse items, read descriptions & reviews on sites they’re already familiar with. Since they're the same items from the same sellers, Dema offers the identical return and refund policies on the site that shoppers are already accustomed to.&#x20;

After shoppers select items, add them to their Dema cart, checkout and pay, Dema places that money into escrow. After all, the money cannot be sent to the seller, if the role of the marketplace is to arbitrate between the two parties.
